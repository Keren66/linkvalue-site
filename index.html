Questions, answered.

How is LinkMind different from a general-purpose AI chat tool?

LinkMind is built for organizations. Answers draw on a customer's own knowledge bases with source references, access follows administrator-managed permissions, and work runs through governed workflows with activity records.

Which models does LinkMind use?

Models are routed by task – fast models for routine work, stronger models for drafting and reasoning, and embeddings for retrieval. Models run on Amazon Bedrock and are available only as options for authorized users. Customers may also bring their own model API access.

Can LinkMind take actions without approval?

No. Results come back for review. LinkMind does not send email without user action, and consequential business actions stay with authorized people.

Where is data processed?

Workloads and data for Southeast Asian tenants stay in the AWS Asia Pacific (Singapore) Region. Model and customer-region eligibility is confirmed before enablement.

How do you control who can call which model?

Calls to non-local models require explicit administrator authorization for the user account and are limited to authorized model configurations and assigned application functions.
